# Code of Conduct

Kinlogue contributors are expected to make participation respectful, safe, and constructive regardless of background, identity, experience, health status, or technical perspective.

Examples of welcome behavior include giving specific and evidence-based feedback, respecting privacy, acknowledging trade-offs, and helping others learn. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, sexualized attention, and publishing another person's private or medical information are unacceptable in project spaces or related public interactions.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile without including real medical records or other sensitive attachments. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not this conduct channel.
